# Cognitive Fitness OS

A decision framework for integrating AI into knowledge work while preserving human judgment.

Rather than treating AI as a single tool, this operating system assigns distinct responsibilities to each component of the workflow. The goal is not to generate more content—it is to improve thinking, execution, and learning.

---

## Core Philosophy

AI should amplify thinking, not replace it.

- Humans define the problem.
- Perplexity researches the external world.
- ChatGPT manages thinking.
- Espanso operationalizes repeatable workflows.
- Claude produces polished deliverables.
- Humans make the final judgment.

---

## Operating Model

```text
Problem
   │
   ▼
Pen & Paper
Clarify the problem and develop an initial point of view.
   │
   ▼
Perplexity
Research what the world currently knows.
   │
   ▼
Pen & Paper
Develop an independent perspective.
   │
   ▼
ChatGPT
Think, synthesize, critique, and design the workflow.
   │
   ▼
Espanso
Execute standardized prompt architectures and reusable workflows.
   │
   ▼
Claude
Produce exceptional long-form deliverables.
   │
   ▼
ChatGPT
Review, critique, extract durable learning, and identify improvements.
   │
   ▼
Pen & Paper
Compress, internalize, and decide.
```

---

## Responsibilities

### Pen & Paper

**Purpose**

Develop understanding.

**Best for**

- ambiguity
- first principles
- systems thinking
- defining the problem
- memory
- intuition
- final judgment

---

### Perplexity

**Purpose**

Research operating system.

**Best for**

- current information
- industry research
- comparisons
- external evidence
- citations
- validating assumptions

---

### ChatGPT

**Purpose**

Thinking operating system.

**Best for**

- synthesis
- context management
- planning
- prioritization
- critique
- decision-making
- prompt architecture
- workflow design
- extracting durable learning
- identifying contradictions

ChatGPT manages the workflow rather than simply generating content.

---

### Espanso

**Purpose**

Execution operating system.

**Best for**

- reusable prompt architectures
- workflow automation
- standardized execution
- reducing repetitive work
- operational consistency

Espanso transforms proven workflows into repeatable systems.

---

### Claude

**Purpose**

Production operating system.

**Best for**

- long-form writing
- portfolio artifacts
- documentation
- strategic synthesis
- polished deliverables
- structured reports

Claude turns well-defined thinking into exceptional output.

---

## Design Principles

Every workflow follows the same philosophy:

- AI should amplify thinking, not replace it.
- Separate thinking from production.
- Automate execution, not judgment.
- Build workflows instead of prompts.
- Preserve human review before publishing.
- Capture durable learning after every significant output.
